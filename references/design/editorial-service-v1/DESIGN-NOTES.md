# Editorial Service Website v1 — visual reference

**Status:** inspiration only; not a reusable template or an approved client deliverable.

The accompanying screenshot should be saved as `desktop.png` in this folder.

## Why we selected it
- Confident, readable editorial headlines and high-contrast dark/light sections
- Owner-led story and real-work photography as visual anchors
- Service features using images rather than a wall of identical cards
- Illustrative phone conversation showing the customer experience
- Clear inspection/contact actions and distinct page pacing

## What to adapt, not copy
Apply the underlying ideas of visual hierarchy, story, purposeful mockups, and conversion clarity. **Do not copy** the reference brand's palette, branding, copy, images, exact layouts, customer messages, or graphic treatments into another client site.

## Quality caveats
- Verify all imagery rights, business claims, reviews, media mentions, and credentials before publication.
- Phone messages shown in mockups must be labeled as illustrative if they are not actual approved communications; do not imply a live automation exists.
- Preserve semantic SEO, mobile readability, accessibility, performance, and useful service-page links.

## How to use
During new-client design planning, inspect this screenshot **alongside** the client's actual brand/assets and other references. Explain which general design principles are useful and how the new design will be visually distinct. Never treat it as a source project to copy.
