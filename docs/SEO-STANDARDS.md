# SEO STANDARDS

Use the imported SEO skills for deeper analysis. These are Book Moar defaults.

## Core
- One clear search intent per primary page.
- Unique page titles and meta descriptions.
- Logical H1-H6 hierarchy.
- Descriptive URLs.
- Canonical URLs must be intentional and correct.
- Include sitemap and robots.txt.
- Avoid duplicate thin location/service pages.
- Do not create doorway pages.
- Do not stuff city names or keywords unnaturally.

## Local Service Sites
Where justified by real service coverage and useful content:
- service pages
- service-area pages
- FAQs
- trust signals
- reviews
- local proof
- project/gallery content

## Internal Linking
Follow docs/INTERNAL-LINKING.md.

## Schema
Use appropriate schema only when supported by real page content and verified facts.

## Content Integrity
Do not fabricate:
- reviews
- locations
- awards
- certifications
- experience
- prices
- guarantees

Use context/SOURCE-OF-TRUTH.md as the factual authority.
