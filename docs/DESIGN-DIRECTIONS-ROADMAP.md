# Book Moar — Future Design Directions

**Status: planning only.** Do not build six templates, add dependencies, or force a preset style onto current client projects.

## Why this exists
As Book Moar completes real client websites, capture successful design approaches as reusable **directions**, not rigid clones. Every direction must comply with [BOOK-MOAR-STANDARDS.md](../BOOK-MOAR-STANDARDS.md): verified above-the-fold trust, obvious contact/quote actions, original branding, accessibility, technical/local SEO, performance, and safe migration practices.

## Candidate directions (not approved templates)

| Bold & Trusted | Confident contrast, clear credentials, prominent CTAs | Pest control, HVAC, roofing |
| Clean & Modern | Spacious, restrained, contemporary | Professional services |

| Industrial & Rugged | Strong type, durable materials, job-site photography | Contractors, automotive |

These are hypotheses, not a fixed client-to-style mapping. Select based on each client's actual brand and audience.

## Adoption gate
1. **Now:** prioritize Crystal and other real client projects. Do not generate style-library code.
2. **After 3–4 strong, reviewed sites:** identify repeated design patterns and document which actually worked. Do not claim conversion lifts without data.
3. **Then:** propose a small set of reference directions with screenshots, typography, palettes, spacing, photography guidance, responsive examples, and clear differentiation.
4. **Only with approval:** extract reusable primitives/components into the starter without importing another client's content, assets, or proprietary brand.
5. **Ongoing:** keep a client-specific design brief and explicit reasons for choosing or departing from a direction.

## Invariants vs variables
**Always shared:** semantic SEO foundations, crawlability, structured data accuracy, responsive accessibility, performance budgets, clear lead capture, verified trust, safe DNS/email preservation, QA.

**Always customized:** colors, typography, layout, imagery, voice, service hierarchy, trust presentation, and contact experience.

## Design review record (fill in later)
For each candidate: client/project (with permission), screenshots desktop/mobile, business context, design rationale, components worth extracting, measured performance, SEO validation, accessibility results, and observed conversion data if available.

**Do not imitate Go-Forth Pest Control or any other reference site.** Extract principles such as prominent authentic social proof and immediate contact access, never its distinctive composition, graphics, or branding.
