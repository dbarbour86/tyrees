## Rule
Never invent or assume:
- years in business
- certifications
- licenses
- awards
- guarantees
- pricing
- review counts
- service areas
- employees
- ownership claims
- response times
- availability
- financing
- "best", "#1", "top-rated", or similar claims

If a fact is not verified here or in a cited client source, treat it as unknown.

## Claim Scope Guardrail — Never Broaden a Verified Fact
A verified fact may be paraphrased for clarity, but its factual scope must never be expanded to create a stronger marketing claim.

Examples:
- "no chemical fertilizer" does NOT authorize "chemical-free," "no synthetic sprays," "pesticide-free," "herbicide-free," or "organic"
- "degree in dairy management" does NOT authorize a different degree, certification, or professional credential
- "family-owned" does NOT authorize a specific number of generations unless verified
- "pasture-raised" does NOT authorize "grass-fed," "grass-finished," "organic," or "free-range" unless separately verified
- "serves [city]" does NOT authorize a larger service area unless verified

When a source is narrower than the desired copy, preserve the narrower claim. Never fill the gap with a plausible inference.

Before publishing a factual marketing claim, classify it as:
- VERIFIED — explicitly supported by an approved source
- SAFE PARAPHRASE — restates a verified fact without changing or expanding its meaning
- UNSUPPORTED — no reliable source found
- INFERRED — plausible, but not explicitly supported

Only VERIFIED and SAFE PARAPHRASE claims may be published. Rewrite or remove UNSUPPORTED and INFERRED claims.

## Source Rules
Business-controlled sources may be used to verify facts, including the official website and official business social profiles such as Facebook and Instagram.

Record the source for important facts whenever practical. A fact found only on an official social profile may still be used, but do not silently treat it as if it came from the website.

For contact information, verify the exact phone number, email, address, or booking URL against a business-controlled source before publishing it. If sources conflict, flag the conflict for human review instead of choosing one.
