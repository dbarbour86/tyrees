# CLIENT RESEARCH: TYREE'S AUTO DETAILING

## 1. Customer Psychology & Search Intent
- **Customer Mindset:**
  - Car owners often feel embarrassed by neglected interiors (spills, dog hair, crumbs) or dull oxidized paint.
  - They lead busy lives (commuting, parenting, long work weeks) and lack 6–8 hours and commercial equipment to detail properly.
  - They have had bad experiences with $15 automatic car washes (scratched clear coat, lingering grime, dull water spots).
- **Core Buying Triggers:**
  - Selling or trading in a vehicle (needs showroom prep for top dollar).
  - Bringing home a new baby or driving family/clients.
  - Seasonal refresh (spring pollen cleanup, post-summer road trip, winter salt prep).
  - Special vehicle care (high-end enthusiast cars, RVs before travel season, boats before lake season).
- **Key Objections & How Tyree's Resolves Them:**
  - *Objection:* "How long will this take?" -> *Answer:* Honest expectation: full detail takes 6–8 hours.
  - *Objection:* "Do I need to be stuck at home?" -> *Answer:* We come to your home or office. If you don't have shade or power, we pick up and drop off.
  - *Objection:* "Will the price change suddenly?" -> *Answer:* Starting prices are clear; condition adjustments (e.g. heavy pet hair) are reviewed upfront before we begin.
  - *Objection:* "Can I just get a quick maintenance wash?" -> *Answer:* Maintenance is $60–$95, reserved for existing customers to maintain our quality baseline.

## 2. Competitive Landscape (Clayton & Triangle NC)
- Many competitors are either franchise mobile vans with entry-level technicians, or fixed-location brick-and-mortar shops that require leaving a car all day with no pickup option.
- Tyree's stands out with 20+ years of direct personal craftsmanship, flexibility between mobile on-site and shop pickup/drop-off, and verified multi-vehicle expertise (cars, trucks, boats, RVs, horse trailers).
